        <p>We use Plausible Analytics to understand how people use our website and improve its content. Its reports summarize information such as pages visited, referral sources, browser and device types, approximate location and engagement with the site.</p>
        <p>Plausible does not use analytics cookies or persistent visitor identifiers, and it does not track visitors across websites or devices. It processes an IP address and browser information to calculate statistics without storing the raw IP address or browser user-agent string. These analytics practices are separate from information you choose to provide through a contact or appointment request. Learn more in <a href="https://plausible.io/data-policy">Plausible’s data policy</a>.</p>
